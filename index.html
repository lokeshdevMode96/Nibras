The seven design principles
  1. Value before questions. Help first, personalise later.
  2. Learning data is never performance data.
  3. Earn adoption. Success is people choosing to return.
  4. Attempt before answer. For learning, try first, then get feedback.
  5. Approved sources, always cited.
  6. Stable, accessible interface. Fixed components, not free-form screens.
  7. Depth matches task risk.

What the validation will confirm

  1. Is the Learning Agent licensed at DoF, and which licences does it need?
  2. Can instructions enforce attempt before answer and shape feedback?
  3. Can sources be restricted, ranked and cited?
  4. Can persona and department apply beyond the learner profile?
  5. Can it surface on the portal and in the mobile app?
  6. Can reporting stay private to the learner and aggregated for programme design?
  7. What are the indicative people, licence and hosting costs of each path?
